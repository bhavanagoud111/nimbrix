# Forecasting and Anomaly Detection

All models are deterministic, statistical, and evaluated offline before use. Code:
`apps/api/src/nimbrix_api/modules/ml/`.

## Features (`daily-cost-v1`)

- Daily UTC series per scope (`total`, `service`, `account`, `team`, budget scopes), from the
  first day with data to `as_of` (last complete day), up to 180 days of history.
- Days without records inside that range are spend **0** and counted as `missing_days`.
- **Training only:** values beyond ±3.5 scaled MADs of the same-weekday seasonal baseline are
  winsorized so a one-off spike does not distort the level. Detection and evaluation use raw data.
- Splits are leakage-safe: every fold trains only on data before its origin
  (`test_backtest_never_trains_on_test_data`).

## Forecasting

| Model | Version | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seasonal naive (repeat last week) | `seasonal-naive-7d-v1` | Baseline, always evaluated |
| Damped additive Holt-Winters, weekly seasonality | `hw-damped-additive-7d-v1` | Alternative: level + trend + weekly seasonality, cheap and interpretable |

**Evaluation:** rolling-origin backtest, 6 origins 7 days apart, horizons 7 and 30 days.
Metrics: MAE, WAPE (Σ|error| / Σ|actual|), and prediction-interval coverage.

**Selection:** Holt-Winters is used only when its backtest WAPE is lower than the baseline's;
the stored metrics always include both models and `improvement_vs_baseline_pct`.

**Intervals (80% default):** cross-conformal: per horizon step, the
`ceil((n+1)·L)/n` quantile of absolute backtest errors. Coverage is estimated by
leave-one-fold-out. With six folds the correction makes intervals conservative.

**Insufficient data:** < 21 days → the run is stored with `status = insufficient_data` and
no points. 21 days up to (horizon + 28) days → baseline only, with intervals from
week-over-week residuals, explicitly labeled "coverage unverified".

Each forecast stores model name/version, feature version, training window (`training_start`
… `training_end`), horizon, interval level, points (`yhat`, `lower`, `upper`), and metrics.
The analysis job reuses a forecast already trained through the current `as_of` instead of
recomputing it.

## Anomaly detection (`seasonal-residual-v1`)

For each scope and day *t*:

- `expected(t)` = median of the same weekday in the previous 4 weeks (≥ 2 required);
- `scale(t)` = 1.4826 × MAD of residuals over the previous 28 days, **excluding days already
  flagged**, floored at max(5% of expected, $1);
- flagged when `(observed − expected) / scale ≥ threshold` **and**
  `observed − expected ≥ max($5, 20% × expected)`.

| Sensitivity | Threshold |
| --- | --- |
| low | 5.0 |
| medium (default) | 3.5 |
| high | 2.5 |

Consecutive flagged days form one anomaly window. Re-detection extends the existing
anomaly (dedupe on detector + scope + overlapping window) and never overrides a human status.
Lifecycle: `open → acknowledged → resolved | dismissed`, reopen allowed; invalid transitions
return 409. Evidence: daily observed/expected/score (evidence IDs `cost:<scope>:<date>`),
baseline method, the cost query, and top contributing resources (`resource:<id>`) with
"new in window" flags. Only increases are detected.

## Measured results

Generated by `uv run python -m nimbrix_api.modules.ml.evaluation`
(raw output: [`docs/results/ml-evaluation.json`](results/ml-evaluation.json)).
Hardware: Apple M4, 24 GB RAM, macOS; Python 3.12.12, statsmodels 0.15.0. Run date 2026-10-10.

**Detector**, 200 synthetic series × 150 days, 400 labeled events (one- to three-day spikes and
level shifts of 0.25–1.5× the base level), weekly seasonality, trend, 2–6% noise:

| Sensitivity | Precision | Recall | False positives / 1,000 days | Mean latency (days) | Largest missed event |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| low | 1.000 | 0.945 | 0.000 | 0.045 | 0.68× base |
| medium | 0.987 | 0.980 | 0.167 | 0.026 | 0.42× base |
| high | 0.968 | 0.980 | 0.433 | 0.018 | 0.42× base |

**Forecasts**, 40 clean synthetic series × 180 days, 6-fold rolling origin, 80% intervals:

| Horizon | Model | MAE | WAPE | Interval coverage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 7 days | seasonal naive | 11.62 | 4.51% | 83.3% |
| 7 days | Holt-Winters | 8.75 | 3.40% | 83.3% |
| 30 days | seasonal naive | 12.47 | 4.81% | 83.3% |
| 30 days | Holt-Winters | 9.00 | 3.50% | 83.3% |

**Demo dataset:** the injected `search-reindex-batch` spike is detected on its first day for
the `AmazonEC2` service, account `333333333333`, team `search`, and total scopes, with the 8
new instances as top contributors, and no other anomalies (`test_demo_spike_is_detected_with_evidence`).

**Caveat:** these are synthetic series whose generating process matches the model
assumptions; real billing data (irregular batch jobs, credits, month-end charges) will score
lower. The numbers demonstrate correct, reproducible behavior, not production accuracy.
